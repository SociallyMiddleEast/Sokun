Our philosophy

The quiet that the mountain gives you, made into a practice.

Bikfaya has always been where Beirut comes to breathe — cool air, pine shade, slower mornings. Sokun brings that feeling indoors: warm stone, soft light, unhurried hands, and time that belongs only to you.

Every visit is built around one idea: you should leave lighter than you arrived.
