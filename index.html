About Sokun

Wellness is how you move, and how you eat.

Sokun — سكون — means calm. It's the feeling after a good class: heart still racing a little, mind finally quiet.

From the power of boxing and Cross Fit to the flow of belly dance and dabke, the focus of Reformer Pilates and the lightness of aerial yoga, every class meets you at your level — and there's a full programme for kids. Our diet clinic connects the work you do in the studio with what's on your plate.
